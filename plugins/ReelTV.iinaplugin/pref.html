Enter the full folder path; paths beginning with ~/ are not supported. A folder containing your shows — one subfolder per show works best, but flat folders and mixed layouts are detected too. Episodes are matched from names like Show S02E03.mkv, Show.2x04.mp4, or Show.S01.E05.avi. Everything stays local; the plugin scans at most three levels deep.
Without a key the library still works fully — you just get plain tiles instead of posters and episode titles. Saved poster images may continue to load from TMDB after the key is removed. Get a free key at themoviedb.org/settings/api. Poster and episode-title requests go directly to TMDB.
